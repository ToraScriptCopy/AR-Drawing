# **AR Drawing**

An ad-free, privacy-respecting, and lightweight camera overlay application designed for effortless tracing, sketching, and physical drawing.

## **The Problem with Play Store Alternatives**

Most "AR Drawing" applications currently available on the Google Play Store share the same fundamental flaws:

* They are bloated with invasive SDKs, trackers, and telemetry.  
* They bombard users with unskippable video ads and aggressive subscription prompts.  
* They require unnecessary system permissions.  
* They lag, consume excessive battery, and overheat the device.

This project was built to solve this. It is a utility-first, stripped-down tool designed to do exactly what you need, with maximum performance and zero compromise on your privacy.

## **How It Works**

The core mechanics are straightforward and highly efficient:

1. **Select an Image:** Choose any photograph, illustration, or sketch template from your device gallery.  
2. **Camera Overlay:** The app opens your camera feed and overlays your selected image on top of it with adjustable transparency.  
3. **Mount and Calibrate:** Place your mobile device on a stand, tripod, or any makeshift support overlooking your canvas (paper, canvas, wall, etc.).  
4. **Trace & Draw:** Look at your screen to see both your physical hand/pencil and the reference image aligned, allowing you to trace the outline with pixel-perfect accuracy.

## **Installation**

Since this application respects your privacy and bypasses Google's closed ecosystem distribution networks, it is distributed directly as an APK file.

### **Prerequisites**

To install the application, you must allow installation from unknown sources on your Android device.

1. Go to **Settings** \> **Apps** \> **Special app access** \> **Install unknown apps**.  
2. Select your browser or file manager and toggle **Allow from this source** to enabled.

### **Step-by-Step Setup**

1. Download the latest .apk file from the [Releases](#bookmark=id.d3u5w4if619o) section of this repository.  
2. Locate the downloaded file in your device's file manager.  
3. Tap on the file and follow the system prompts to complete the installation.  
4. Launch **AR Drawing** and start sketching.

## **System Requirements**

* **Operating System:** Android 8.0 (Oreo) or higher.  
* **Permissions:** Camera access (required solely to render the live background feed for tracing).
